A newspaper front page that sums up Friday, 20 November 2026 in one
image. The lead story is the first-ever three-way summit in Shenzhen,
where the leaders of the United States, China and Russia issued a joint
four-point peace declaration. Below it: the World Court's 9-6 ruling
that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; Anthropic, the
maker of Claude, listing on the Nasdaq as ANT and closing its first day
up about 17%; Houthi missiles striking Riyadh's financial district and
damaging the PIF tower; Ukraine hitting the Kerch Bridge for a third
time in four days as Kyiv is struck again; and world markets falling
for a fifth straight day, with the Nikkei down 4.8% and SoftBank down
over 16%. A strip at the foot adds three more stories from the day:
the Iran war entering its ninth month, the US Senate rejecting an Iran
war-powers resolution 51-48, and Kenya discharging its last Ebola
patient. Every story is drawn as a flat pictogram with a short headline
and caption, on a clean front-page grid, with the date written out in
full at the top. The layout is one wide lead panel for the summit, three
panels for the court, the IPO and Riyadh, two wide panels for the Kerch
Bridge and the markets, and an "Also today" strip with three more
stories from the same day.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This copies `news.png` (built by `build_image.py`, pure Python 3.13
standard library, no network) to `out/entry.png`. Run it twice and the
two outputs are byte-identical.
